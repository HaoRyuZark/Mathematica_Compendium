# Mathematica Compendium

`Mathematica Compendium` is a personal collection of notes from my lectures in applied mathematics and computer science, pure mathematics and 
personal self-study. After some time the idea came to just make this public, so that everyone who may like my style of explanation can read this 
and use it as an assistance for his or her studies.

Some important notes: 

- As stated before, I am still student and thus, the notes mostly from lectures and some proofs are from exercises or self-study; hence, errors are possible

- English is not my native language. I made my best to write this document the best I could and I used LLMs plus spellcheckers for error correction, but they 
sometimes do not catch all the errors.

--- 

## Goal 

The primary **goal** of this document is to work as quick-reference when working on mathematical exercises or proofs at an academic education level. It is not 
intended as a replacement for math-text books used at the university. Again, is written mostly for myself, but it may help you ;)

## AI Usage Policy

For me **AI** is a multiplier and assistant. I do not use it for giving me the: definitions, explanations, proofs, etc. I did used AI during the development of this 
project, but mainly for:

- Transforming a copy-pasted test from a book into latex code, I reviewed.

- Writing exercises/proofs I already solved on paper.

- Do structural refactor like transforming subsections into sections in multiple files. 

- Correct my English.

- Help me structure the tikz pictures.

- Accelerating the writing process when a suggestion of copilot for latex-code was identical to what was in the book. 

For all other cases, I typed every single character, since I fully support *human work*.
